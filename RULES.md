# Simon — Rules (authoritative source of truth)

_Simon: the memory arcade. Watch the pads light up, then repeat the sequence._

## 1. Objective
Repeat the growing sequence of flashing pads without a mistake. Each
survived round adds one step to the sequence and the playback gets faster.

## 2. Setup
- 4 chunky physical pads, each with its own distinct tone
  (pad 1: E4 329.63 Hz, pad 2: G4 392.00 Hz, pad 3: C5 523.25 Hz, pad 4: E5 659.25 Hz).
- The engine starts round 1 with a 1-step random sequence.
- Modes: Solo, Endless, Score Attack. Difficulties: Chill, Classic, Turbo.

## 3. Turn order
1. Engine flashes the full sequence, one pad at a time (light + tone).
2. Player repeats the sequence by tapping pads in the same order.
3. Survive → next round: one new random step appended, playback speeds up.
4. Mistake → handled per mode (see §7).

## 4. Legal moves
- Tapping any pad during the INPUT phase.
- A tap is correct when it matches the next unrepeated step of the sequence.

## 5. Illegal moves
- Taps during WATCHING, COUNTDOWN, ROUND CLEAR, PAUSED, or GAME OVER are
  ignored elegantly — they never affect the sequence or score.
- Tapping the same pad twice in a row is legal only if the sequence says so.

## 6. Captures
Not applicable (solo memory game).

## 7. Special rules
- **Speed:** playback is 62% lit / 38% gap per step. Step time shrinks each
  round: Chill starts 800 ms (floor 340), Classic starts 640 ms (floor 220),
  Turbo starts 480 ms (floor 150).
- **Solo:** one wrong tap ends the game.
- **Endless:** the player has 3 lives. A wrong tap costs one life and the
  engine replays the SAME sequence (no new step). Losing all 3 ends the game.
- **Score Attack:** 90-second clock. Every correct tap scores 1 point. A wrong
  tap costs 5 seconds and restarts the current sequence attempt. The clock
  running out ends the game.
- **Pause:** freezes sequence playback, the echo light, and the attack clock;
  resume re-enters the current phase exactly.

## 8. Scoring
- Solo / Endless: score = rounds survived.
- Score Attack: score = correct taps within 90 seconds.
- Best round per mode and best attack score persist across sessions.

## 9. Winning conditions
There is no final win — the goal is to beat your best round / score.
Surviving 8+ rounds is "elite" territory.

## 10. Draw conditions
Not applicable (solo game).

## 11. AI strategy
Not applicable — the sequence is generated by a uniform random source.
(No bot opponent in Simon.)

## 12. Edge cases
- Pause during playback: the watchdog resumes the exact step on unpause.
- App backgrounded mid-game: engine freezes; audio pauses and resumes.
- Rapid taps: each tap echoes with a 180 ms light on its own timer, never
  desyncing the phase timer.
- Wrong pad flashes red briefly; the run's result is recorded once.

## 13. Test cases
1. Round 1: engine flashes exactly 1 pad; player repeats → round 2 begins.
2. Wrong tap in Solo → game over immediately; score = rounds survived.
3. Wrong tap in Endless with lives left → life lost, same sequence replays.
4. Wrong tap in Score Attack → −5 s, sequence attempt restarts, game continues.
5. Clock hits 0 in Score Attack → game over with tap score.
6. Pause during playback, then resume → playback continues from the same step.
7. Tap during WATCHING → ignored, sequence unaffected.
8. Speed check: round 20 Classic playback is faster than round 1, never below floor.
9. Stats: best round updates only when beaten; persists across restarts.
